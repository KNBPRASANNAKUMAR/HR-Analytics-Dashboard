📌 **Project Overview**

The **HR Analytics Dashboard** is a comprehensive Power BI project developed to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, departmental performance, and salary distribution within an organization.

This dashboard enables HR teams and business leaders to monitor employee retention trends, identify workforce challenges, and optimize organizational decision-making through interactive analytics and KPI-driven reporting.

The report transforms raw HR operational data into actionable business insights that support workforce planning, employee engagement, and retention strategies.


🎯 **Problem Statement**

Employee attrition and workforce inefficiencies can significantly impact organizational productivity, operational costs, and long-term business performance.

Organizations often struggle to identify the underlying causes of employee turnover and workforce dissatisfaction due to fragmented HR reporting systems and unstructured employee data.

This dashboard was developed to solve the following business challenges:

- Analyze employee attrition across departments
- Identify workforce distribution trends
- Evaluate salary slab impact on employee retention
- Monitor workforce demographics by age and gender
- Understand workforce satisfaction patterns
- Support HR decision-making using business intelligence


🧠 **Business Objectives**

- Analyze employee attrition trends across departments
- Monitor department-wise employee distribution
- Evaluate the impact of salary slabs on attrition
- Identify workforce demographics by age and gender
- Track employee satisfaction across different job roles
- Build an executive-level HR reporting dashboard


🔍 **Key Insights**

- The **Sales Department** recorded the highest employee attrition count.
- Employees within lower salary slabs showed significantly higher attrition rates.
- Most employees belonged to the **26–35 age group**.
- Workforce distribution was heavily concentrated within Operations and Administration departments.
- Employees with lower experience levels demonstrated higher turnover patterns.
- Department-level analysis revealed workforce imbalance across business units.


🧹 **Dataset Cleaning & Analysis**

 🛠️ **Data Cleaning Process**

The dataset underwent multiple preprocessing and transformation steps to improve reporting quality and analytical accuracy.

Key cleaning operations included:

- Removing duplicate employee records
- Handling missing demographic values
- Standardizing department naming conventions
- Creating salary slab segmentation
- Converting experience fields into structured numerical categories
- Formatting workforce metrics for KPI reporting

📈 **Data Analysis Performed**

The following analytical processes were implemented:

- Employee attrition rate calculations
- Department-level workforce analysis
- Salary slab segmentation analysis
- Employee age-group distribution analysis
- Gender-based workforce analysis
- Experience-based attrition trend analysis
